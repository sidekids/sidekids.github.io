SideTube

Curated video for a child's first phone

What it is

Parents review and approve channels, videos and playlists — the child chooses freely within that selection. No feed, no trends, no shorts, no comments, no autoplay. New content is never visible until an adult has approved it.

SideTube exists twice: an iOS app (SwiftUI, in active development) and an Android app (a key-navigation fork of the open-source app YouTubeWhitelist).

iOS – Usage & Features

Features (iOS)

  • Per-child profiles with age bands (3–5, 6–8, 9–11, 12+)
  • Editorial workflow: new videos start as "review required"; parents approve, reject or defer — with an audit trail
  • Source trust levels: trusted children's sources, trusted series, per-video review, parent-only, blocked
  • Categories such as Knowledge, Nature, Technology, Stories, Drawing Manga (8+), Anime & Manga (12+)
  • Home with "Continue watching", channels and recently watched; library; search limited to approved content
  • Click-wheel remote (haptic, VoiceOver-friendly) as an optional bottom sheet
  • Sleep timer with volume fade, daily time limit, parent PIN with lockout
  • Recommend a video to family via Messages or Signal — behind a parental gate
  • Autoplay off by default; everything stored locally, no accounts, no tracking

Features (Android)

  • Per-child profiles, approved "subscriptions", continue-watching, T9 search
  • Daily time budgets, quiet hours, sleep timer with volume fade, watch-time statistics
  • Local parent account (no Google login)

Why This Project Exists

YouTube hosts much of what children genuinely want to watch — buried in an attention machine. SideTube keeps the content and removes the machine.

Technical Details

iOS: Swift · SwiftUI · SwiftData · WebKit (official YouTube IFrame player) · iOS 17+

Android: Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · Material 3 · multi-module (MVVM) · Room

Data sources: YouTube oEmbed / RSS first, YouTube Data API as fallback — read-only, no write actions

Status: iOS in active development (TestFlight planned); Android fork feature-complete, on-device test pass pending
