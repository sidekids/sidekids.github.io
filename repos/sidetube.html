The principle

The good stays. The attention machine goes.

YouTube hosts much of what children genuinely want to watch. SideTube turns it into a small, protected space: parents review channels, videos and playlists in advance.

Then children choose on their own. No endless swiping, no trends, no comments. A tool that stops when the task is done.

Everyday use

Clear for children. Accountable for parents.

For children

A clear place with familiar categories and a search that only finds approved content.

  • Continue watching and library
  • Channels and playlists
  • Click-wheel control
  • No comments or trends

For parents

A traceable approval process instead of a black box. Decisions can be explained, changed and talked about together.

  • Approve, reject or defer
  • Age bands and trust levels
  • Parent PIN, daily limit and sleep mode
  • History of approvals

For families

SideTube sticks to the essentials and treats privacy as a technical default.

  • Stored locally
  • No accounts and no tracking
  • Autoplay off by default
  • Open source under GPL-3.0

To be clear

What SideTube does not replace.

Technology does not replace accompaniment. Approvals, daily limit and sleep mode help keep an agreed frame; younger children still need adults who watch along, answer questions and talk about what they saw. Approvals are not complete protection: approved channels publish new videos, product placement inside a video cannot be filtered out, and the embedded YouTube player may show ads depending on the video.

Age bands are presets, not ratings. SideTube's age bands help parents choose. They are not legal age ratings and not assessments by FSK or any other rating body; most YouTube videos carry no official age rating. Official film ratings are themselves not educational recommendations. Whether a video fits is the parents' decision, ideally after watching it together.
