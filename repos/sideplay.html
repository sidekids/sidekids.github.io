The principle

Offline first.
No subscription needed.

A child's audio plays and favourite music should not require a streaming subscription, a commercial account or a permanent connection. SidePlay plays the family's own collection and downloads it to the device; after that, everything works without a network.

Resume takes one tap, audio plays are grouped by series, and search only finds what is approved in the family's own library. When a story ends, it ends.

Own server

Plex today.
Nextcloud as an alternative.

Today SidePlay reaches the family's media collection through Plex: audio plays, music and audiobooks live on a server on the home network, and SidePlay fetches them from there and keeps them on the device for the road.

Nextcloud is planned as an alternative: music from the Nextcloud "Music" app via its Subsonic interface, with a separate read-only key per device. Families who already use Nextcloud for photos and files will not need a second media server. This source is not available yet.

Everyday use

Simple for children.
Clear for parents.

⌂

For children

Large tiles, few paths and a home screen that continues with whatever played last.

  • Resume with one tap
  • Favourites and own lists
  • Audio plays by series
  • Sleep timer with fade-out
◇

For parents

Settings sit behind a parent lock and can be explained to the child.

  • Parent lock with PIN
  • Show or hide libraries
  • Minimum age per title
  • Downloads on Wi-Fi only
◌

For families

SidePlay plays what is already there and sends nothing out.

  • Data stays on the device
  • No SideKids account
  • Own server instead of a platform
  • No recommendations, no tracking
