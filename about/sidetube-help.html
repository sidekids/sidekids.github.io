SideTube help

Setup, Screen Time and common questions.

Getting started

  1. Set a parent PIN on first launch.
  2. Create a profile for the child.
  3. In the profile, load the starter pack or paste the link of a channel, a playlist or a video.
  4. Under “Review approvals”, decide what the child may watch.
  5. Tap “Lock” to switch to kid mode. The lock and the PIN lead back to the parent area.

Apple Screen Time: two requests instead of one

With Apple's Screen Time set up on the child's iPhone, the child may have to ask for time for SideTube first and later once more for a website. The reason: SideTube plays videos in the official YouTube player. iOS also counts this player as website use. SideTube cannot change that.

To avoid that, add SideTube under “Always Allowed” in Screen Time and manage viewing time in SideTube itself: quiet hours and daily limit are in the child's profile.

“Bedtime” appears in the evening

Quiet hours are switched on from 20:00 to 06:30 in every new profile. The parent PIN lifts the lock until quiet hours end. Times and the switch are in the profile under “Quiet hours”.

A channel shows no videos

At times YouTube does not deliver a channel's list. Individually approved videos and the videos of the starter pack still play. Every playback needs an internet connection. A family's own YouTube key is the lasting remedy.
