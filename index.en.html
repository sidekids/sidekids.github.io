The idea

Media literacy starts
with the right frame.

Digital education is not about keeping children away from media. It is about creating an understandable space where they can explore, choose and gradually take responsibility.

01⌁

Accompany,
don't monitor

Parents set the frame. Children get room to make their own decisions.

Gradual autonomy ↗
02◒

Fewer stimuli,
more focus

No feeds, trends or autoplay: the tool waits instead of demanding attention.

The Less Online idea ↗
03♢

Private
by default

Local data, open software and no business model built on child profiles.
